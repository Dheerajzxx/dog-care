FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?
Walk-ins are welcome during opening hours, but booked appointments are seen first. For emergencies, come straight in or call (555) 910-0911.
What should I bring to my first visit?
Any previous medical records or vaccine booklets, a list of current medications, and your dog's favorite treats to make the visit pleasant.
Do you handle insurance?
Yes — we work with all major pet insurers and provide detailed invoices for claims. Payment is due at the time of the visit.
How early should I arrive?
About 10 minutes before your appointment, especially for a first visit so we can set up your dog's profile.
Can I request a specific veterinarian?
Absolutely — pick your preferred doctor during online booking, or mention it when you call.
