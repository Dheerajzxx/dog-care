Know the signs

Emergency warning signs

If you see any of these, call first — we'll guide you while you're on the way.

🫀 Collapse or seizures

Fainting, uncontrolled shaking, or unresponsiveness needs immediate care.

🤢 Repeated vomiting

Especially with blood, or attempts to vomit with nothing coming up (bloat risk).

🩸 Bleeding

Bleeding that doesn't stop within 5 minutes of pressure.

🦴 Trauma

Hit by a car, big fall, or any impact injury — even if your dog seems fine.

🌬️ Breathing trouble

Labored breathing, choking sounds, blue-tinged gums.

🍫 Poisoning

Chocolate, xylitol, grapes, medications or unknown substances.

While you're on the way

Stay calm — dogs read your energy. Keep your dog warm, still and restrained (box or blanket stretcher for spine injuries). Call ahead so the ER team preps a bay before you arrive.

Bring any packaging if you suspect poisoning, and note the time symptoms started.

What to tell us on the phone

  • What happened & when
  • Your dog's breed, age, weight
  • Symptoms you're seeing right now
  • Any medications or allergies on file
