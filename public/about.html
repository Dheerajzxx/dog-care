About PawCare

Family-owned since 2011 — a hospital where wagging tails are the best vital sign.

🏥🐾
Our story

Started by vets who grew up with dogs

PawCare began as a two-room clinic in 2011. Today we're a full-service dog hospital with surgical suites, dental stations, an on-site lab and Springfield's only around-the-clock canine ER.

We stay small on purpose: our vets know every patient by name — and every patient knows the treat jar.
